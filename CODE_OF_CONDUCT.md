# Code of Conduct

<!-- Maintained by the project maintainers. Scope: this repository, its issue tracker, pull requests, and any community space attached to the project. -->

## Our pledge

We want participation in Creativemountain Liveness to be a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community — including the community around the
board game table, where this game is actually played.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people, including people you
  disagree with about the rules, the balance or the wording of a card.
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences. A balance argument is
  a technical argument: it is settled with measurements, not with volume.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback. When the gate chain
  rejects a change, that is the mechanism working, not a personal judgement.
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience. This project records its mistakes publicly in
  `docs/沉淀/经验教训索引.md`; nobody is expected to be perfect.
- Respecting the physical and social nature of the game: it is played face to
  face, often with people who have never seen the code.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when
an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces. It
covers:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions for this repository.
- Any community space the maintainers create around the project (chat, forum,
  mailing list, events).
- Interactions at the table when the game is played as part of a community
  event, and any offline gathering organised around the project.

## AI-assisted contributions

Contributions drafted with AI assistance are welcome, with three conditions:

1. **You own the contribution.** If you open a pull request, you can explain
   every line of it, including the parts a model suggested, and you can run the
   gate chain and interpret its output.
2. **You verify behaviour, not wording.** A patch that looks right is not
   evidence. A change to the engine, a theme pack or a gate needs a negative
   sample (violate ⇒ red, restore ⇒ green) or a measurement showing the
   behaviour changed, as described in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
3. **You disclose it.** State in the pull request that AI assistance was used
   and what it did (drafting, refactoring, test scaffolding). This is not a
   disqualification; undisclosed AI output is, because it makes review
   impossible.

Generated content that is not verified this way may be closed without prejudice.
If you are unsure whether a contribution is acceptable, open an issue and ask
before writing the code.

## Moderation responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards, and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to
any behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
Maintainers have the right to remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code,
issues and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct,
and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the project maintainers through the repository owner's GitHub
profile, or by private message in the project's community space. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obliged
to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will apply these Community Impact Guidelines when determining the
consequences for behaviour they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct.

| Level | Community impact | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Correction | Inappropriate language or other behaviour deemed unprofessional or unwelcome | A private written warning, clarifying why the behaviour was inappropriate; a public apology may be requested |
| 2. Warning | A violation through a single incident or a series of actions | A warning with consequences for continued behaviour; no interaction with the people involved for a specified period; may lead to a temporary or permanent ban |
| 3. Temporary ban | A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behaviour | A temporary ban from any public interaction with the community for a specified period; may lead to a permanent ban |
| 4. Permanent ban | A pattern of violation, including sustained inappropriate behaviour, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals | A permanent ban from any public interaction within the community |
